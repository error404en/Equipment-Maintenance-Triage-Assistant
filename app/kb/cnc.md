# Spindle Inspection
If spindle noise is reported during rapid movement or spindle acceleration, a full inspection is required. Check the bearings and lubrication.

# Coolant Temperature
The expected coolant temperature during normal operations is 18 to 24 degrees Celsius. If the temperature is outside this range, inspect the chiller unit.

# Vibration
Vibration can indicate misaligned axes or worn bearings. Monitor vibration levels during high-speed operations.
